Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Overview

VUTASO QR Generator ("we", "our", "the Service") at vutaso.com/qr is a client-side web application that lets you create QR codes directly in your browser. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit the content you enter (URLs, WiFi passwords, contact details, etc.) to any server. All QR code generation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript.

Analytics

We may load privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible) to understand aggregate usage (page views, feature events). Analytics does not receive your QR content. Requests go to plausible.io when enabled.

Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage on your device only:

  • Theme preference (light / dark)
  • Language preference (English / Vietnamese)
  • Recent QR history (type, encoded payload, style snapshot)
  • Custom color themes you save
  • Logos you embed in saved themes or history (stored as images on this device only)

This data never leaves your device. Clearing site data in your browser removes it.

Third-Party Services

The Service loads Font Awesome icons from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. QR libraries, the color picker, export tools, and Inter fonts are self-hosted on this site. CDN providers may log your IP address when your browser requests their files.

Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies. Third-party CDNs or analytics may use cookies according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
